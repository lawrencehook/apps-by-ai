# Historical collections for interactive projects

A field guide to material that could support an interactive archive, from an executable sequence of moves to a drawing whose geometry still has to be reconstructed. The middle column describes what the source provides. The third column suggests an interaction to build. The publication date of a database or survey can be much later than the observations or objects it records.

## Patterns, performances, and play

| Material | Available form | An interaction it could support | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Church bell ringing | The Central Council maintains a library of named methods. A method specifies how the bells change order from one row to the next. | Ring one bell yourself while the others play their parts. Trace where you move in the order. | [Central Council of Church Bell Ringers](https://cccbr.org.uk/resources/methods/) |
| Reproducing piano rolls | Stanford distributes raw and expression MIDI and rendered audio derived from rolls. | Switch between two renderings of a passage to hear what the expression encoding contributes. | [Stanford Piano Roll Archive downloads](https://supra.stanford.edu/download/) |
| Japanese Go | Downloadable SGF game records include archives for historical players such as Honinbo Dosaku. | Replay a game stone by stone, hide the next move, then reveal what the player chose. | [A. E. Brouwer’s game archive at CWI](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/index.html) |
| Baseball scorekeeping | Retrosheet has game-level CSVs going back to 1897, with play-by-play and deduced events where available. Coverage varies by game. | Rebuild the field after each recorded play and show which states were deduced. | [Retrosheet downloads](https://retrosheet.org/downloads/csvdownloads.html) |
| Court dancing | Pécour’s printed duets encode paths, steps, and bar lines synchronized with the music. Digitized pages. | Scrub a musical bar to see where two dancers cross. | [Library of Congress duets](https://www.loc.gov/item/44029074/) |
| Broadside ballads | Single-sheet songs survive with lyrics, tune references or music, and woodcuts. The Bodleian directs readers to its SOLO catalog. | Follow one tune or recurring woodcut through sheets telling different stories. | [Bodleian ballad collections](https://www.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/collections-and-resources/special-collections/finding-aids-and-catalogues/ballads) |

## Mechanisms, construction, and working drawings

| Material | Available form | An interaction it could support | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering and architectural surveys | HABS, HAER, and HALS document more than 40,000 sites and structures across the combined collection, with measured drawings, photographs, and reports. The mix varies by site. | Register a section against a photograph, then move the cutting plane through a reconstruction. | [Library of Congress collection](https://www.loc.gov/item/2009632512/) |
| Timber bridges | The Deer’s Mill covered bridge survey documents a specific Burr arched truss. | Peel away the siding and trace one timber from an elevation into the assembled bridge. | [HAER survey drawings](https://www.loc.gov/resource/hhh.in0209.sheet/?sp=5) |
| Ship hulls | The Smithsonian catalogs watercraft plans and sells reproductions of many. Some HAER vessel surveys have digitized body plans, sheer plans, and half-breadths online. | Select one station in the side view and watch its cross-section take its place in the hull. | [Smithsonian ship plans](https://americanhistory.si.edu/about/departments/work-and-industry/ship-plans) · [Example HAER drawing set](https://www.loc.gov/resource/hhh.ca3458.sheet?st=gallery) |
| Mechanical linkages | Cornell’s Reuleaux collection includes photographs, dimensions, descriptions, and related models. Its Peaucellier record identifies 39 related straight-line mechanisms. | Turn the crank and trace successive joints on different models. | [Cornell’s Peaucellier linkage](https://digital.library.cornell.edu/catalog/ss%3A372718) |
| Steam pumping engines | The Deer Island engineering report identifies individual cylinders and documents the valve drive, with references to specific photographs. | Turn the shaft by hand and watch the corresponding valve lift. Internal dimensions would need separate verification. | [Deer Island engineering record](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/ma/ma1400/ma1439/data/ma1439data.pdf) |
| Paper astronomical instruments | Peter Apian’s sixteenth-century astronomy book contains layered rotating discs used to find planetary positions. The exhibit identifies 21 woodcuts with these devices. | Rotate a facsimile disc while keeping the printed scales and cutouts visible. | [University of Chicago Library](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/collex/exhibits/book-use-book-theory-1500-1700/dimensional-thinking/coordinating-time/) |
| Mechanical tide prediction | William Thomson’s 1872 predictor survives with ten adjustable components and pulleys. The museum provides an object record and photographs. | Engage one pulley at a time and see how its movement changes the combined curve. | [Science Museum Group](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co53901) |

## Craft, material culture, and everyday life

| Material | Available form | An interaction it could support | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Andean khipus | The Open Khipu Repository distributes a SQLite database of recorded cords, knots, colors, and relationships. | Reconstruct one recorded object in 3D. Select a cord to see which attributes were observed and where its branches attach. The geometry can be explored even where meaning remains uncertain. | [Open Khipu Repository](https://github.com/khipulab/open-khipu-repository) |
| Weaving | The Bronsons’ 1817 handbook contains more than thirty numbered drafts with cording and treadling directions. Digitized pages. | Step the loom from the draft and trace one diamond in the fabric back to its instructions. | [Bronson weaving handbook](https://www.handweaving.net/textile-documents/8421/the-domestic-manufacturer-s-assistant-and-family-directory-in-the-arts-of.html) |
| Dye sample books | One public-domain trade book has 54 mounted dyed fabric swatches and instructions. Full-size images and a PDF are downloadable. | Put the printed recipe beside the actual surviving swatch. Display color can be presented as a reading of the scan. | [Science History Institute](https://digital.sciencehistory.org/works/cnlv4q9) |
| Early modern recipes | The Folger pairs digitized recipe manuscripts with volunteer transcriptions and reading copies. | Compare the order of operations in several versions of one dish, then open the corresponding handwritten line. | [Folger recipe books](https://folgerpedia.folger.edu/About_our_recipe_books) |
| Restaurant menus | NYPL documents CSV exports and linked menu, page, item, and dish records, including prices where recorded. | Give someone the price of a meal in a particular year and let them order from an actual menu. | [NYPL menu data documentation](https://nypl.github.io/menus-api/) |
| Museum objects | The Met offers a collection CSV and CC0 images for public-domain works under its Open Access initiative. | Compare the same kind of tool, vessel, or instrument across makers and centuries. | [The Met Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access) |

## Places, journeys, and historical observations

| Material | Available form | An interaction it could support | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ships’ weather logs | CLIWOC release 2.1 contains digitized observations from ship logbooks, 1750–1854, with a downloadable dataset. | Sail one recorded passage day by day and watch the observed wind change along the route. | [PANGAEA dataset](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.743343) |
| Fire insurance maps | The Library of Congress indexes Sanborn maps by place and supplies digitized sheets for public-domain editions. | Pick one block and compare building footprints and construction symbols across survey years. | [Sanborn map guide](https://guides.loc.gov/fire-insurance-maps/sanborn-searching) |
| Railways and postal routes | Historical maps and timetable holdings preserve routes and scheduled stops. Online access varies by title. | Ask whether a passenger or letter could make a particular connection using one dated timetable. | [Library of Congress railroad guide](https://guides.loc.gov/railroads/maps-timetables) |
| Roman coin hoards | Oxford’s project has a searchable database of hoards and their recorded contents. | Open one find and compare the ages and origins of coins buried together. | [Coin Hoards of the Roman Empire](https://chre.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/search/) |
| Local Irish folklore | The Schools’ Collection gathers accounts written down by schoolchildren in the 1930s, with manuscripts, place information, and transcribed entries. | Follow one weather sign, riddle, or place-name story across neighboring schools. | [Dúchas Schools’ Collection](https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbes/stories) |
| Tree rings | NOAA archives raw ring-width measurements and site chronologies. These are modern measurements of growth reaching back before instruments. | Slide two sequences of wide and narrow rings past one another to see how cross-dating works. | [International Tree-Ring Data Bank](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/paleoclimatology/tree-ring) |
| Kyoto cherry blossoms | Researchers compiled dates of full flowering from historical records extending back to the ninth century. Coverage is intermittent. | Open a calendar to the same week across centuries and see which years have a recorded bloom. | [Kyoto flowering data and source notes](https://katatagenki.cloudfree.jp/wp/wp-content/themes/katatalab/document/index.html) |
| Chinese star charts | The Dunhuang atlas represents more than 1,300 stars on a series of maps. Researchers have studied its projection and identified stars. | Unroll the atlas against a modern sky and select a star that can be matched across both. | [International Dunhuang Project study](https://idp.bl.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/THE-DUNHUANG-CHINESE-SKY_-A-COMPREHENSIVE-STUDY-OF-THE-OLDEST-KNOWN-STAR-ATLAS.pdf) |
| Glass astronomical plates | Harvard’s plate database can be searched by object or sky position and indicates whether a plate has been scanned. | Revisit one patch of sky at successive exposure dates and look for an object whose brightness changes. | [Harvard Plate Stacks](https://platestacks.cfa.harvard.edu/search-dasch) |

## Images that preserve motion or shape

| Material | Available form | An interaction it could support | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multiple-view motion studies | Muybridge’s plates and catalog document successive exposures, camera angles, and time intervals. | Pause a horse mid-stride and switch among the original views. | [University of Pennsylvania archive](https://archives.upenn.edu/digitized-resources/docs-pubs/muybridge/) |
| Early film and recorded sound | The Library of Congress presents Edison company motion pictures and sound recordings; many films survive through paper prints. | Scrub an industrial film frame by frame to see the order of a worker’s movements. | [Inventing Entertainment](https://www.loc.gov/collections/edison-company-motion-pictures-and-sound-recordings/about-this-collection/) |
| Fossils and prehistoric artifacts | Smithsonian Human Origins points to 3D collections for fossils and artifacts. Here the measured surface geometry has already been captured. | Rotate two objects into the same orientation and compare a particular ridge, edge, or wear surface. | [Smithsonian 3D collection](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/3d-collection) |

## Where the material is most usable

For a quick experiment, **bell methods, SGF games, piano-roll MIDI, and ships’ logs** already contain ordered events or observations. **Khipus** are a rarer find for 3D work because the downloadable data describes the structure of individual physical objects. **Hull plans and mechanical drawings** make the geometry itself the interesting work. A useful first test is whether two independent views agree on the same cross-section.

Access ranges from downloadable data to scans, collection guides, and plans sold as reproductions. Reuse terms belong to the particular dataset or digitized item. The Science History Institute sample book and The Met Open Access collection are two examples with explicit public-domain or CC0 material. The [earlier project sketches](archive-project-ideas.md) develop five of these directions in more detail.
