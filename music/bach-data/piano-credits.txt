Salamander Grand Piano
Yamaha C5 grand piano recorded by Alexander Holm.
https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-grandpiano/

The MP3 files come from the Tone.js audio collection.
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander

The distribution credits Alexander Holm under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/blob/master/salamander/README
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

This edition embeds 20 original MP3 files in Base64 for offline access. Playback transposes nearby samples and applies a release when each note ends.
