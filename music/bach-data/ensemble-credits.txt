Local edition
The organ uses the loud manual registration. Violin, viola and cello use sustained
ensemble recordings with vibrato. Each note is downmixed to mono, resampled at
24 kHz, trimmed, level-balanced, and crossfaded into a sustain loop. The FLAC
audio is embedded in a local script so playback works offline. Pitch centers
come from the publisher's SFZ mappings.
