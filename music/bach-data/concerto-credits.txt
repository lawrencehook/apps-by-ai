Brandenburg Concerto No. 5 instrument recordings

The flute, solo violin, and double bass come from Versilian Studios' VS Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell with sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. The double bass plays the violone line.
https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/tree/440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e

The English Zuckermann kit harpsichord comes from the Versilian Community Sample Library's normal register.
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e
https://versilian-studios.com/vcsl/

Both sample libraries are released under CC0 1.0.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The local samples are trimmed, level-balanced, and downmixed to mono FLAC at 24 kHz. Bowed strings and flute have crossfaded sustain loops. The harpsichord retains the recorded pluck and decay. The ripieno strings use the additional recordings listed in ensemble-credits.txt.
