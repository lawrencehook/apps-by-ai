Jeongganbo digital editions

The four original YAML scores and TteoiJeonggan font come from Tteoieonire's
Jeonggan editor, revision 4fd735aac3aa9f86923a188fa018adf2e0cf3a0d.
https://github.com/Tteoieonire/Jeonggan/tree/4fd735aac3aa9f86923a188fa018adf2e0cf3a0d

The score transcriptions and their adaptation in scores.js are distributed
under GPL-3.0 (GPL-3.0.txt). The YAML files here are the original editable
editions. The local audio readings use the editor's interpretation of
relative notes, tempo, rests and ornaments. Instrument choice, balance
and dynamics are editorial.

Melody: Alexander Holm's Salamander Grand Piano, CC BY 3.0. Pitched,
mixed and rendered from the samples used by this site's Bach collection.
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Janggu strokes: National Gugak Center, Korean Traditional Music Digital
Sound Library. Six sampled strokes as processed by Tteoieonire, mixed
according to each edition’s drum cycle. Korea Open Government License
Type 1 (attribution).
https://github.com/Tteoieonire/Jeonggan/tree/4fd735aac3aa9f86923a188fa018adf2e0cf3a0d/assets
https://www.kogl.or.kr/info/license.do
