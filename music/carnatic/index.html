The five elements

In these five Sanskrit songs, Dīkṣitar (1775–1835) addresses Śiva at five South Indian temples. Each temple is associated with an element, and each song has its own rāga, the melodic framework that shapes a performance.

Subbarāma Dīkṣitar published the Saṅgīta Sampradāya Pradarśini in 1904. P. P. Narayanaswami’s 2023 Malayalam edition gathers 221 compositions by Muttusvāmi Dīkṣitar from that work. The notes above the syllables and the marks around them describe melodic movement. The catalogue gives the rhythmic cycle (tāḷa) printed at the head of each score. Five-element group in the edition
