Wigley hull

Naval architects tow scale models through water to study resistance. The Wigley model has a particularly simple underwater shape: its horizontal slices and transverse sections are parabolas. Move the section from one end to the other to see how those curves form a hull.

Submerged hull · 8 m model

Drag or use arrow keys to rotate

Geometry follows the 8 m towing model documented by the Society of Naval Architects of Japan in 1972. The beam is 0.80 m and the draft is 0.50 m. The amber face marks the selected section.
